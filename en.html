HodoHodo

A Chrome and Edge extension that blocks the sites you choose, such as video or social media, by hours of the day or by a daily limit such as 30 minutes. Shortly before a site is blocked, a bird walks along the bottom of the page to let you know.

We are preparing to publish it on the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons. Installation steps will appear here once it is available.

What it does

  • Add the sites you want to limit, and set a daily limit and blocked hours (for example, weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00) for each one.
  • Add the site you're on with the HodoHodo button in the toolbar. The popup shows how many minutes you have left today.
  • Shortly before a site is blocked, a "Blocking in … min" bar appears at the bottom of the page, and birds walk in (see "The notice before blocking" below).
  • Need a little longer? Press "Extend 5 min" in the popup. You can do this up to 3 times a day, across all sites.
  • When you open a blocked site, birds are waiting for you, with an old proverb or a few words from the bird.
  • See how many minutes you spent on each site today, and a 7-day chart, on the settings page.
  • Don't want the birds? Turn on Simple mode, and notices appear as text only.

Time is counted while the browser window is in front and the active tab is one of the sites on your list. If you are inactive for 2 minutes, it keeps counting only while that tab is playing sound (for example, while you just watch a video). Muting the tab does not stop the count if a video is still playing. Background tabs and time while your screen is locked are not counted.

The notice before blocking

From 1 minute before a site is blocked (you can choose 3 or 5 minutes, or Off, in the settings), a text bar and walking birds are shown at the bottom of that site's tabs. The closer it gets, the more birds appear. When the time comes, the tab switches to the blocked page.

  • HodoHodo does not read the page (its text, what you type, or what you're viewing), and does not change the page's text or colors.
  • The bar and the birds are only drawn on top. Your clicks still go through to the page underneath.
  • It appears only on tabs of sites you added and allowed. Access is asked for one site at a time, when you add that site.
  • The bird pictures are bundled with the extension. Nothing is loaded from outside.

Harder to unblock

To loosen the settings of a site that is blocked right now (raise its limit, change its hours, remove it, and so on), you wait 60 seconds first. While you wait, the bird asks, "Are you sure you want to loosen it?" It never scolds you. This is on for everyone from the start, including the free version.

The birds

The bird in the HodoHodo icon is an American woodcock, from North America. It walks slowly, rocking its body back and forth. Never in a hurry, just enough. I took that walk as the model.

You can choose from these 9 birds on the settings page. Choose several, and they appear together on the blocked page and in the notice before blocking. In the toolbar popup, one of them appears each time you open it.

American Woodcock, Long-tailed Tit (Shima-enaga), Java Sparrow, White Java Sparrow, Tree Sparrow, Budgerigar, Cockatiel, Penguin, Shoebill

Please note
