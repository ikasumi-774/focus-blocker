Focus Blocker

A Chrome and Edge extension that blocks the sites you choose, such as video or social media, by time of day or by a daily time limit.

We are preparing to publish it on the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons. Installation steps will appear here once it is available.

What it does

  • Add the sites you want to limit, and set a daily limit for each one. When you use up the time, the site is blocked for the rest of the day.
  • Create schedules by day and time (for example, block on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00).
  • When you open a blocked site, a page shows when it will open again.
  • See how many minutes you spent on each site today on the settings page.

Time is counted while the browser window is in front and the active tab is one of the sites on your list. If you are inactive for 2 minutes, it keeps counting only while that tab is playing sound (for example, while you just watch a video). Muting the tab does not stop the count if a video is still playing. Background tabs and time while your screen is locked are not counted.

Please note

  • You can always turn off or remove the extension itself. It helps you stick to your limits, but it cannot guarantee that you won't overuse a site.

  • It is not a tool to get around school or workplace network restrictions. It only blocks the sites you add yourself.
